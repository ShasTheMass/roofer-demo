Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer emergency roof repairs?

Yes, we run an on-call emergency service for storm damage and active leaks, with most call-outs attended within a few hours.

Are you insured and accredited?

We're fully insured with £5m public liability cover and are members of the National Federation of Roofing Contractors (NFRC).

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes — we provide detailed reports and photo evidence to support storm and accidental damage claims with your insurer.

What guarantee do you offer?

All full re-roofs come with a 10-year workmanship guarantee. Repairs are guaranteed for 12 months.
